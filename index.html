CMS Migration AEM Sitecore

Enterprise CMS Migration: Sitecore → Adobe Experience Manager

Accenture Inc.  ·  2022–2025  ·  ACCENTURE.COM/CAREERS

The Challenge

Migrate 1,000+ pages of enterprise content from a legacy Sitecore CMS to Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) — without breaking existing URLs, losing content, or disrupting live digital properties across multiple regions.

My Role & Approach

  • Worked within a cross-functional team of 10 across content, dev, and QA over 12+ months.
  • Managed content mapping and page-by-page migration into AEM's component architecture.
  • Tackled the most complex challenge — URL redirect management — coordinating with developers to ensure zero broken links post-launch.
  • Conducted thorough QA checks at every migration milestone to catch errors before go-live.
Go-live on schedule Fewer post-launch errors 1,000+ pages migrated Team of 10
